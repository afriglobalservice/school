Brownies


🟤Fudgy Chocolate Brownies Ingredients


½ cup (115g) unsalted butter, melted

1 tbsp cooking oil (olive or vegetable oil)

1⅛ cup (225g) granulated sugar

2 large eggs

2 tsp vanilla extract

½ cup (65g) all-purpose flour

½ cup (50g) unsweetened cocoa powder

¼ tsp salt

Method

Preheat your oven to 175°C (350°F).

Lightly grease an 8-inch square baking pan and line it with parchment paper.

In a medium-sized bowl, combine the melted butter, oil, and sugar. Whisk well until combined.

Add the eggs and vanilla extract. Beat the mixture for about a minute until the color becomes slightly lighter.

Sift in the flour, cocoa powder, and salt.

Using a rubber spatula, gently fold the dry ingredients into the wet ingredients until just combined (do not overmix).

Pour the batter into the prepared baking pan, smoothing the top with your spatula.

Bake for 20-25 minutes. A toothpick inserted in the center should come out with a few moist crumbs attached, but not wet batter.

Let the brownies cool completely in the pan before lifting them out and slicing.
