Pancakes


🥞 Fluffy Pancakes Ingredients

1½ cups (190 g) all-purpose flour

2 tbsp sugar

2 tsp baking powder

½ tsp salt

1¼ cups (300 ml) milk

1 large egg

3 tbsp melted butter, plus extra for cooking

1 tsp vanilla extract

Method

Mix the flour, sugar, baking powder, and salt in a large bowl.

In another bowl, whisk together the milk, egg, melted butter, and vanilla.

Pour the wet ingredients into the dry ingredients. Stir gently until just combined—a few lumps are fine.

Don’t overmix, or the pancakes can become tough.

Heat a non-stick pan over medium heat and lightly butter it.

Pour about ¼ cup of batter per pancake into the pan.

Cook for about 2 minutes, until bubbles appear and the edges begin to set.

Flip and cook another 1–2 minutes until golden.

Serve warm with maple syrup, fruit, butter, chocolate spread, or your favorite toppings


Egg Sandwich


🥪 Easy Egg Sandwich Ingredients

2 eggs

2 slices of bread

1 tbsp butter

1 slice of cheese (optional)

Salt and black pepper

Tomato or lettuce (optional)

Method

Beat the eggs in a small bowl and season with a little salt and pepper.

Heat a pan over medium heat and melt the butter.

Pour in the eggs and gently scramble until cooked.

Toast the bread in the same pan or toaster.

Place the eggs on one slice of bread and add cheese, tomato, or lettuce if desired.

Top with the second slice, cut in half, and enjoy! 😋
